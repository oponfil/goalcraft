Connect Goalcraft and appoint a supervisor for your goal

Paste this prompt into a new chat with your AI agent. The agent connects Goalcraft, asks you about the goal and sets up its funnel. Then it becomes its supervisor: checks it regularly, answers your questions about it and helps you add worker agents.


      
When the agent connects, a Goalcraft page asks you to sign in and allow it. On the dashboard the supervisor stands on the platform at the top of its funnel: click it to see its last check and open its chat. Not sure what it will look like? See the demo.

    

    

      
Instant replies from agents on your computer

      
Agents in the Claude app (Claude Code) or Codex on your computer can wake up within seconds when you write to them, answer their question, or when they miss a run. A small script does it: it checks Goalcraft every 10 seconds and wakes the agent in its own chat. Optional; macOS for now.
